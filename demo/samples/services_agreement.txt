MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made effective as of January 15, 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Acme Corp ("Supplier") and Globex Ltd ("Customer").

1. SERVICES
The Supplier shall provide the services described in Schedule 1. The Supplier must deliver all deliverables within thirty (30) days of each order. The Service Level Agreement defines uptime targets.

2. PAYMENT TERMS
The Customer shall pay each invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt. Fees are USD 12,500 per month. Late payment incurs a penalty of 1.5% per month.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION
This Agreement shall expire on January 14, 2029 unless terminated earlier. Either party may terminate for material breach.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party agrees to keep Confidential Information secret.

5. LIABILITY
The Supplier's liability is limited to fees paid. Neither party is liable for indirect damages.

6. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Maharashtra. Disputes shall be resolved by arbitration; the courts of Mumbai have jurisdiction.
